# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version
may change how things work.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- Twilight in a corner of the screen: she strolls, talks, dances when music plays, dozes off late at night and can be picked up and thrown.
- The sprites come from a script that fetches them, with the credits; the folder they are in is a setting.
- Settings in `mascot.toml` and a page for them in the settings window.
